About AI Design LA

Why AI Design LA exists

Design is changing. We should shape what comes next.

AI is changing more than the tools designers use. It is reshaping the products we create, the roles we play, and the way teams move from idea to something real.

AI Design LA brings together designers, researchers, design engineers, product thinkers, and creative technologists to learn from one another, share what is working, and question what is not.

There is no established playbook yet. That is why we are building one together.

What we explore

The new tools, practices, products, and questions shaping design

AI-native design workflows

How AI changes research, ideation, prototyping, production, and the way design teams collaborate.

Designing AI products and agents

New interaction models, adaptive systems, conversational experiences, and products that can act on a user's behalf.

Vibe coding and design engineering

Using tools like Claude Code, Cursor, Lovable, and Figma Make to move from concept to working software.

AI-powered design systems

Making components, tokens, guidelines, and product patterns more useful to both people and AI.

Teams, leadership, and culture

What it takes to help designers learn new skills, change old processes, and become more effective with AI.

Trust and human experience

Designing for transparency, control, safety, accessibility, and the messy realities of human behavior.
