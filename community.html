What the community is making

Prototypes, workflows, experiments, and things worth sharing

AI Workflow

Research synthesis in 20 minutes

Yuna Takahashi

Key takeaway

Claude + structured interview notes + a reusable prompt template. The analysis still requires a human, but the first pass takes minutes, not days.

Design System

Token schema for LLM consumption

Marcus Webb

Key takeaway

Naming conventions and structured metadata that let AI code generators produce on-brand components without a designer reviewing every output.

Prototype

Streaming UI states in Figma

Leila Ahmadi

Key takeaway

A plugin for prototyping non-deterministic loading states — the kind you get from streaming LLM responses. Available free on the Figma community.

Research

When AI transparency backfires

Priya Nair

Key takeaway

Three rounds of user testing on explaining AI reasoning in product UI. Short finding: showing the "why" helps at first and starts to erode trust if overused.

Case Study

A brand built with AI tools

Devon Castillo

Key takeaway

Full brand identity for a real client, documented step-by-step. Every tool used, every prompt that worked, and the three directions that were complete dead ends.

Playbook

Shipping AI features responsibly

Sam Okafor

Key takeaway

What to do when the feature might be wrong — error states, fallbacks, user communication. Open-sourced and now used by multiple product teams.

Community Voices

Real answers to real questions — not testimonials

What has AI changed about how you work?

"I stopped treating the first draft as sacred. AI makes it so cheap to get a rough version that you spend almost all your time on the part that actually matters — the judgment call."

Priya Nair

Senior Product Designer

What are you still skeptical about?

"The idea that AI makes everyone a designer. It makes everyone able to produce something that looks designed. That's not the same thing, and I think we'll spend years untangling the difference."

Marcus Webb

Design Engineer

What are you trying to learn next?

"How to do research on AI products when the AI itself changes between studies. Every time a model updates, you're not quite studying the same thing. Nobody has figured this out yet."

Yuna Takahashi

UX Researcher

What should designers be paying more attention to?

"Error states. Every AI feature fails differently, and most of us are shipping products where failure is a common path, not an edge case. We're not designing for that seriously enough."

Sam Okafor

Product Lead

Community Principles

How we treat each other and why it matters

01

Be respectful

Treat everyone with kindness and professionalism. Debate ideas, not people.

02

Share generously

Post your work, experiments, workflows, tools, and lessons learned. We all benefit when people contribute.

03

Stay curious

Everyone is at a different stage with AI. Ask questions, share knowledge, and help others learn.

04

Keep it relevant

Focus discussions on AI, design, product development, UX, research, engineering, and related topics.

05

Respect confidentiality

Don't share confidential information from your employer, clients, or unreleased products.

06

Keep promotion balanced

Sharing your projects, events, and tools is welcome when it's relevant and contributes to the community. Avoid spam or excessive self-promotion.

07

Help build a great community

Be welcoming, inclusive, and collaborative. The culture of this group is something we create together.
