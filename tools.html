Why This List Exists

The useful tools earn their place

Not everything is worth your attention

New AI tools appear every week. Most do not deserve a permanent place in a designer's workflow. This is a deliberately selective list of products that members of the AI Design LA community have used enough to form an honest point of view about.

How We Classify Them

What the labels mean

Core Stack

Tools people regularly depend on. Tested across enough real projects to trust.

In Rotation

Useful for particular workflows, projects, or moments — but not every one.

Watching

Promising, but not yet proven enough for regular use. Worth keeping an eye on.

Dropped

Tools that were replaced, declined, or created more friction than value.
